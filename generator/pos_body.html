    Questions shop owners ask.

    Do I need new hardware?

    It runs on a tablet or laptop you may already own. To take cards at the counter you’ll want a Square Terminal, bought directly from Square.

    Who holds my customers’ card details?

    Square does. Our system stores only Square’s receipt IDs and amounts, never card numbers.

    Can I change it later?

    Yes. Turn add-ons on or off from month to month, and ask for changes as your shop grows.

    What if I want to leave?

    Cancel any time. We export your products, customers and sales history to spreadsheets you keep.

    Is it just for retail?

    It’s built from modules, so it fits shops, groomers, salons, studios and market vendors. Tell us how you work and we’ll show you the fit.
