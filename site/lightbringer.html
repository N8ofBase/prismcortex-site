Prologue: Terror on Talus

Terror on Talus

I was witness to The Scorch that initiated the Fear Rift. I watched from a distance as the planetary headquarters of Talus was decimated by the Falling Star known as Starvos. I am using the last remaining energy from our Life Tree to send this knowledge beacon and trigger the implementation of Project Lightbringer.

My name is Machiventa Melchezidech, and grand rotations past, I had the honor of training with the most wonderful beings; the corps of volunteers under the steady administration of Lanaforge, the Planetary Protector, at the magnificent yet simple planetary headquarters of Talus: Turantia. I was taught advanced techniques in agriculture, medicine, governance, art and leisure; as well as the true nature and purpose of the universe, which allowed me to connect to my All Spirit and begin my true celestial journey of Love.

With my new found connection to The Creator, I traveled the towns of Talus spreading the Love of Icosa and the wisdom of Turantia.

A few grand rotations after I left Turantia, "The Falling Star" obliterated this city of Light, breaking the connection Talus had with Icosa and the rest of the loving universe. Thus I am compiling this history of Talus, The Scorch, and the wonderful beings that inhabit this world for the coming of The Lightbringer. First, I will outline some key terms to know regarding the physics, evolution, and celestial hierarchy of Talus and Icosa.

Being the 20th personification of the Grand Creator is a source of honor for Icosa, and thus weaves that number into the existence of their experimental evolutionary worlds. In many worlds, like Talus, time for mortals flows according to Chrona Icosa, or Icosa Time. Compared to the flow of time from your last adventure it looks like this:

1 sectick = 3 seconds

1 mintick = 20 secticks/60 seconds

1 trintick = 20 minticks/20 minutes

1 tick = 3 trinticks/1 hour

20 ticks = 1 rotation/1 day

20 rotations = 1 planet cycle/1 month

20 planet cycles = 1 grand rotation/1 year

So 1 year, or Grand Rotation, on Talus is 400 days, 20 hours a day, 20 days a month, 20 months a year in relative time.

The 20 planet cycles in a grand rotation are named for the moon or planet that is dominant during that cycle.

Icona, Caspien, Olphine, Silva, Avendwel, Horace, Ego, Distar, Ryovess, Nachuun, Illconus, Clovum, Orkun, Shuntu, Alecra, Hresh, Envereen, Diyaposa, Ryovinn, Nio.

Celestial Hierarchy of the Nebrom System

The Nebrom System has a local headquarters world called Vardoon and it is system number 88 in the constellation of Ashenta. This constellation consists of 100 local systems and has a headquarters world called Onorix. Ashenta is constellation number 20 in the universe of Dalamatia. The local universe of Dalamatia consists of 100 constellations and has a capital known as Revona. The universe of Dalamatia is number 100 in the minor sector of Ensa.

Icosa: The Creator of Talus and Sovereign Administrator of Dalamatia is the personification of the 20th original concept of infinite identity. The headquarters of Icosa is located on Revona.

Life Carrier: These celestial beings are essential to the creation of new evolutionary worlds. They work with the system administrators to select appropriate planets for these life experiments and plant the seed to start the evolutionary process.

All Spirit: This is the piece of The Grand Creator that inhabits all sentient life, destined to fuse with the personality of their mortal in the glorious journey of celestial evolution.

The world of Talus is actually a moon orbiting the planet Tywinn in the crowded Nebrom system. At any given moment there are at least 3 moons or planets on the horizon, sometimes blocking out the rest of the sky. Despite this busyness, it received the perfect amount of light conditions from the system star, known as Neb by the inhabitants of Talus. The star light is not always direct, many times reflecting off of and eclipsed by surrounding planetary bodies, creating magnificent hues and hazes along with the occasional brilliance of magnetic manipulation along the poles of this moon, and was thus chosen to be one of trillions of evolutionary worlds executing the enterprise of experience in an ever expanding, loving universe. This particular universe is governed by Icosa, a celestial being of unconditional love and a thirst for the creative chaos of worlds evolving toward Light and Life. Many millions of Grand Rotations past, a Life Carrier named Nambia was dispatched to Talus. Once the ecological conditions were ideal, Nambia arrived and fuzed with the world, transforming from an ethereal being into a physical, abluza, crystalline tree; it’s roots penetrating the ground and injecting the specially formulated Life Plasm designed for Talus, infused with the Spirit Spark of Nambia, to kickstart the evolution of sentient life.

Over the eons, water and plant life formed, followed by simple single celled organisms that evolved into complex sentient creatures, all with unique features, strengths, and weaknesses that come with the struggle of evolution. The dominant sentient life forms of Talus are the Dimoneesh, Leyf, Lizerus, Melkin, and Sapiens.

The Dimoneesh are highly intelligent creatures with skin tones ranging from pale wheck to deep verado and bleck. Evolving from the Capreeshic species, they have curved horns that stretch back from their head, along with hooved feet. In Talus society they are regarded as great scholars and teachers, as well as being devoted to protecting others from harm.

The Leyf are large creatures that spend much of their life in the trees. Evolving from Bulg species, their slick skin ranges from grerde to brozn helping them blend into their forest homes. In Talus society the Leyf are the caretakers and protectors of the land, known for their superior survival skills and unmatched vegan cuisine.

The Lizerus are strong, proud, and fierce creatures. Evolving from the Lazuron species, they have thick scaly skin ranging in many colors. In Talus society they are excellent warriors, honorable spiritual leaders, and vigilant shop keepers.

The Melkin are the smallest of the sentient creatures on Talus. Evolving from the Eponchi species, they have small, compact bodies, smooth skin ranging from yemillow, brozn, and bleck, with hair of similar color that grows on their head and face. In Talus society the Melkin are master crafters and artists, known for their friendly demeanor and being the first life form to create music.

The Sapiens are the largest of the sentient beings. Also evolving from the Eponchi, the Sapiens are covered in hair ranging in color tones of abluza, anildigo, verado, and often combinations of these. In Talus society, due to their size, they are good warriors, but also hard productive workers known for their farming skill.

Once these life forms had established primitive civilization, it was time for the Planetary Protector to take up residence. The Planetary Protector for the world of Talus is Lanaforge, a mighty and brilliant celestial administrator. Lanaforge, along with 100 volunteers, each from a different evolutionary world, gathered at the system headquarters of Vardoon and were infused with the Life Plasm of Talus to become physical beings and build the Planetary Protector Headquarters on Talus, called Turantia. This simple city was built by the Lanaforge 100 around the Nambia Tree using basic, primitive tools, but all the while connected to the love of Icosa. The purpose of this city is to interact with the confused and primitive life forms of Talus, to guide them in their evolution and show them the truth about the love of the universe. This is not something that happens in one rotation. The beings of primitive worlds must be brought along gradually and are very stubborn and resistant. But time means nothing to the timeless Lanaforge and messengers are sent out to all parts of Talus to spread the truth and recruit beings to come back to Turantia to learn the true nature of the Creators and the Universe.

This mission was going perfectly. These life forms were learning to work together to make their world a better place. Until Starvos came. According to the ancient texts of Dimoneesh scholars, the “Falling Star'' began to appear in the sky on the sixth rotation of Caspien during the 389th grand rotation (2/6/389). They noticed a rojed streak growing larger, closer, rotation after rotation until on Alecra 17, 392 (15/17/392), the falling star crashed into Talus, but more specifically next to Turantia. The impact was so fierce that it eliminated a third of the inhabitants of Talus while Turantia was decimated and plunged upward onto a massive mountain plateau created by the collision, uprooting the Nambia Tree and severing the connection to Icosa and the rest of the Universe. Unfortunately this was only the beginning.

From the ashes of the impact emerged the Death Lazuron Starvos. A massive muscular creature covered in scales made of rock and magma, a gaping maw full of bleck teeth and lava bubbling in its throat. Their claws pierced into the heart of the planet and began siphoning away all life, converting it into unimaginable dark power and Fear. There were many who believed this being to be a god, and worshiped it to gain its power. More, however, would be on the side of life and rise up against this evil. Five heroes equipped with Artifacts attuned to Icosa that weren’t destroyed from the impact, confronted Starvos in a battle for the planet’s soul. Each of these heroes had a unique role with special skills that worked together to defeat Starvos.

Brawler: The role of Brawler is taken up by beings that have mastered the body's balance and energy allowing them to subdue and submit their rivals with a technique called Que Bao.

Champion: The role of Champion is taken up by beings of honor and faith who strive to protect others with a combination of tactics, skill, and weapon mastery.

Maverick: The role of Maverick is taken up by beings with sharp eyes and sharp wit. Able to disable rivals from a distance with breathtaking accuracy.

Soul Seer: The role of Soul Seer is taken up by beings who have connected with the latent energies of Icosa and use it to bend reality and control nature.

Whisper: The role of Whisper is taken up by beings that have mastered stealth and camouflage. Able to move through the shadows unnoticed to take rivals by surprise.

Here are “The Heroes of the Scorch,” as the history books have dubbed them, and the Icosa Artifact each carried to emerge victorious against Starvos.

Sartina, a Melkin Whisper, possesses the Cloak of Shadows imbued with Icosa energy that gives them unmatched speed and reaction resulting in powerful abilities.

Hinon, a Sapien Brawler, possesses the Havona Hammer, a massive Icosa enchanted maul granting unmatched strength and powerful abilities.

Umatu, a Lizerus Champion, possesses the Universe Medallion granting them a great increase to all natural abilities as well as access to divine powers of Icosa.

Rork, a Dimoneesh Soul Seer, possesses the Shield of Grace that protects them from Fear and enhances their Icosa powers.

Yelena, a Leyf Maverick, possesses the HeartShard Bow that never misses its target and imbues arrows with powerful Icosa energy.

With Starvos defeated, the lifeforms of Talus gathered the bodies of their heroes to return to their homes, but their homes had been turned into barren wastelands because of what is now being called The Scorch. In one final selfless act, the heroes gave their life energy to Icosa, turning them into magnificent crystalline trees whose roots spread out and started to transform the burnt landscape into colorful life again. It will take time to bring life back to this scarred planet, but this is a good start. It is now 200 grand rotations after The Scorch and Five cities and towns have blossomed from these "Life Crystals," each being named for the hero that gave their life for its roots.

Hinon is a quiet fishing and farming village populated by Sapiens with their Life Crystal surrounded by lush farmland and gardens. A magnificent statue of Hinon, created by some Melkin artisans, stands above a large tomb where all are buried so Hinon will protect them on their journey to the next life.

Rork has evolved into a fortress made up exclusively of Dimoneesh high in the mountains on the Talon of Talus. After The Scorch, the Dimoneesh of Rork became reclusive and obsessed with studying Starvos and where it came from. Rork contains the most extensive library in the world, but no one else has access to it. Their Life Crystal is in the middle of a grand courtyard protected by wheck stone walls and towers.

Sartina itself is a work of art. From nothing, the Melkin built an intricate city in the face of a cliff with tunnels leading deep underground where their Life Crystal has created a grerde oasis that is used as a communal and recreation area with hot springs and fruit trees.

Umatu is a thriving coastal city populated mostly by Lizerus. Order is kept by the city guard made up of Champions devoted to Icosa, called Lazur Shield, and is home to the first temple of Icosa on Talus, outside of the one in Turantia. The Umatu Life Crystal is located in a cave behind a massive waterfall and is considered a holy place.

Yelena’s Life Crystal fused with one of the last remaining Riverwood trees, transforming it into a gigantic city of trunks and branches populated by the Leyf lifeforms. Underneath the roots of this behemoth is an energy sealed vault containing the last remaining piece of Starvos, its heart. Many attempts were made to destroy it. Rewards were offered, Champions were shamed, and scholars were stumped until an order of Soul Seers, called The Icosahedron, crafted a barrier to imprison the foul heart forever.

For now, life seems to have stabilized for the inhabitants of Talus, but the Fear Rift has been slowly contaminating the world and will soon reach its breaking point. When a Fear Rift appears on a world, it severs the connection that world has with its creator. On top of that, Turantia's wellspring of knowledge, light, and truth was now inaccessible. Without that connection and stability to the love of the universe, the creatures of that world start to succumb to Fear. It starts with the lower life forms, making them selfish and aggressive, turning them into dangerous creatures. This breeds even more Fear and spreads to the higher life forms, thus increasing Fear exponentially until the entire world is a corrupted husk devoid of love. The Fear Rift is completely invisible and unnoticed by the world's inhabitants so you must rely on your connection to Love and Icosa to locate the Fear Rift and seal it before it spreads like a plague to other worlds.

The voice fades away and you are snapped awake from your vision by that small muffled voice you heard before.

"As a LightBringer of Icosa, you have the ultimate honor of inhabiting a special avatar for your adventure on Talus. You will be starting with no abilities, equipment, or experience. Our calculations have us pulling into the orbit of Tywinn and Talus, and settling between the moons Silva and Avendwel. That will give us clear access to portal you onto Talus.”

The voice becomes more muffled and seems to be arguing with another being in the background. The unintelligible mumbling goes on for a few more secticks before going completely silent. You begin to address the voice, but are interrupted with more instructions.

Your first mission is to find the Universe Medallion. Until you find this, we will not be able to contact you. While you cannot ‘die’ as your avatar, you will be stuck in a loop where every ‘mistake’ will return you back to where we teleported you until you find that medallion, it is of the utmost importance. Now that you have been briefed, we will put you into hibernation until we reach our destination, where you will awaken on Talus. Luck be with you, Lightbringer."

Before you have time to question this voice and the whole situation, a fog starts to rise in your small cell and everything goes bleck.

Project Lightbringer

Prologue

Your life flashes before your eyes. Every choice you have ever made. Every milestone, mistake, and ecstasy is happening in real time and somehow all at once. You see all of these moments, not only from your perspective, but from the perspective of all others involved. You feel their feelings as your own, and you realize they ARE your own. You feel connected to everyone, everything. You are one being divided. You can feel a piece of yourself, your true self, in every being on the planet. Each piece is experiencing this world from a different perspective, a shared perfection attempting to experience the imperfection of evolution. As this connectedness washes over you, you melt into the energy of the planet, merging all experiences into one grand experience.

The collective consciousness of the planet expands your awareness beyond this world, beyond this solar system, beyond this galaxy, and connects you to trillions of other worlds, all carrying this piece of perfection connected to you, but also connected to something even beyond this conglomeration of constellations. It is something you cannot seem to understand completely. A feeling so deep and pure that you find yourself falling into it, being consumed by it. Love. Not the love you experienced in your last lifetime, which now feels like another lifetime ago, but a different type of love. Unwavering, unconditional, and unending. You realize that this is the foundation of the universe and that everything, every piece of you spread over trillions of planets and galaxies, is made from and returning to this beacon of love.

You realize that any pain you had, any pain you caused others during the infinitesimal lifetime you had on a primitive planet is forgiven. The weight of your past decisions dissolves as you embrace the infinite identity of the Grand Creator as your own, and your whole being vibrates with the frequency of the universe, shattering you again into a myriad of fragments cascading into new experiences on new worlds. You find your consciousness attached to a perfection fragment hurtling across galaxies until you encounter a comet streaking through space with a dilapidated stone structure barely holding on. With a flash of light, you zip inside the building and find yourself in a physical body on a cold stone slab enclosed in a small room surrounded by stone walls. As you gather your mind that is still reeling from connecting to the love of the universe, you hear a small muffled voice begin to speak in your head.

“Greetings cosmic traveler. You have  chosen to join with Icosa, the Creator of our local universe, to oversee Project Lightbringer. Our castle comet is on its way to the Nebrom system where a Fear Rift has formed on a moon called Talus, cutting off all contact we had with the Planet Protector. We started traveling as soon as it appeared, but it is quite far and we will arrive about 300 Grand Rotations after the Rift has started. In the meantime, absorb the creation and history of Talus through the knowledge beacon to decide what form you will take and how to interact with the other beings.”

A shimmering, prismatic sphere appears to form in front of you and then quickly moves into your face. As the colors swirl, the Talus names of each one suddenly become known to you. At this moment, your mind explodes with a torrent of information about Talus’ creation, creatures, measurements, and celestial hierarchy. Many of these details begin to fade into the back of your consciousness when the images start to slow, revealing visions of fire and upheaval while a somber voice starts to explain the scenes.

Rork contains the most extensive library in the world, but no one else has access to it. Their Life Crystal is in the middle of a grand courtyard protected by wheck stone walls and towers.

Sartina itself is a work of art. From nothing, the Melkin built an intricate city in the face of a cliff with tunnels leading deep underground where their Life Crystal has created a grerde oasis that is used as a communal and recreation area with hot springs and fruit trees.

Umatu is a thriving coastal city populated mostly by Lizerus. Order is kept by the city guard made up of Champions devoted to Icosa, called Lazur Shield, and is home to the first temple of Icosa on Talus, outside of the one in Turantia. The Umatu Life Crystal is located in a cave behind a massive waterfall and is considered a holy place.

Yelena’s Life Crystal fused with one of the last remaining Riverwood trees, transforming it into a gigantic city of trunks and branches populated by the Leyf lifeforms. Underneath the roots of this behemoth is an energy sealed vault containing the last remaining piece of Starvos, its heart. Many attempts were made to destroy it. Rewards were offered, Champions were shamed, and scholars were stumped until an order of Soul Seers, called The Icosahedron, crafted a barrier to imprison the foul heart forever.

For now, life seems to have stabilized for the inhabitants of Talus, but the Fear Rift has been slowly contaminating the world and will soon reach its breaking point. When a Fear Rift appears on a world, it severs the connection that world has with its creator. On top of that, Turantia's wellspring of knowledge, light, and truth was now inaccessible. Without that connection and stability to the love of the universe, the creatures of that world start to succumb to Fear. It starts with the lower life forms, making them selfish and aggressive, turning them into dangerous creatures. This breeds even more Fear and spreads to the higher life forms, thus increasing Fear exponentially until the entire world is a corrupted husk devoid of love. The Fear Rift is completely invisible and unnoticed by the world's inhabitants so you must rely on your connection to Love and Icosa to locate the Fear Rift and seal it before it spreads like a plague to other worlds.

The voice fades away and you are snapped awake from your vision by that small muffled voice you heard before.

"As a LightBringer of Icosa, you have the ultimate honor of inhabiting a special avatar for your adventure on Talus. You will be starting with no abilities, equipment, or experience. Our calculations have us pulling into the orbit of Tywinn and Talus, and settling between the moons Silva and Avendwel. That will give us clear access to portal you onto Talus.”

The voice becomes more muffled and seems to be arguing with another being in the background. The unintelligible mumbling goes on for a few more secticks before going completely silent. You begin to address the voice, but are interrupted with more instructions.

Your first mission is to find the Universe Medallion. Until you find this, we will not be able to contact you. While you cannot ‘die’ as your avatar, you will be stuck in a loop where every ‘mistake’ will return you back to where we teleported you until you find that medallion, it is of the utmost importance. Now that you have been briefed, we will put you into hibernation until we reach our destination, where you will awaken on Talus. Luck be with you, Lightbringer."

Before you have time to question this voice and the whole situation, a fog starts to rise in your small cell and everything goes bleck.

Mission 1: The Universe Medallion

Stranger in a Strange Land

A bright flash awakens you. Your head is spinning from your journey through the universe. You can feel the relevant experiences from your previous mortal life have embedded themselves in your eternal personality. That combined with the still lingering ecstasy of the truth and love of the universe is so overwhelming that you just lay on the hard ground of what you assume is Talus, staring into the norangia hued sky, streaked with lines of dark abluza. Beyond that, a massive, gresh, barren, planet looms with other moons and planets dotting the distance. You even think you can see the little comet that brought you here. Project Lightbringer? So many unanswered questions, you don't even know where to begin.

You start by standing up and testing out your "avatar." It is a strange sensation, but feels natural in this environment as you take a deep breath, smelling the dryness of the ground; a breeze brushes your face, bringing the scent and sound of trees, along with a slight sharp tinge of smoke in the air. It feels good to breathe. You smile. This was not at all what you thought would happen in the afterlife, but here you are, so you decide to embark on this new journey.

As you look around, you see that you are in a clearing of dull, dusty grass. In one direction, you see a dried up creek winding through a sparsely wooded area of brozn trees with rocky mountainous terrain in the distance behind it. In another direction, there is a forest of trees with dark grerde leaves flecked with yemillow veins, a trail of smoke rising above them. You think for a few minticks about your purpose and where to start before deciding to make your way into the forest to investigate the smoke.

After about a trintick of walking through the forest, you start to feel a hunger growing and wonder what kind of things beings eat on this world. Maybe some being is cooking something at the fire creating the smoke, or maybe you can find some nuts or berries in these unique trees, but then you think it is probably not safe to eat random plants from a random world, based on the experience of your last life. The voice from the comet did say that you could not die, but it did not say anything about pain. You pinch yourself hard and wince. Yes, pain is very real in this body.

You have no idea what time of the rotation it is but the Neb light is fading through the trees as you get a little deeper in these woods. You are following the smell of smoke along a somewhat overgrown path, marveling at how different this world is, but still has familiar attributes to the foliage and lifeforms. You notice small insects moving about on the leaves, flowers, and trees; oblivious to this stranger passing by.

While admiring the details of this new forest, you see two glowing rojed orbs hovering behind a plant that has thick large brozn leaves protruding from the ground on sturdy stalks. As you approach the plant, it starts to shudder and the orbs fly toward you, revealing themselves to be the eyes of a putrid bleck and rojed streaked creature with a twisted body of muscle and bone. The creature slams into you, biting your face with a sickening and painful crunch before everything goes dark.

You are jolted awake by a horrific scream echoing through the air and you realize the scream is coming from you. As you quickly stand up, touch your face, and look around, you notice you are back in the clearing where you started. Your face feels a bit numb where that creature attacked, but the pain completely disappeared once back at the arrival spot. You wonder if you went back into the woods if you would find your avatar being eaten by that thing. Does time rewind, or is this a completely new avatar? The sky does not look that much different, but you don't have anything to compare it to. The voice on the comet was speaking the truth. Good to know, but still quite an unpleasant experience that you would like to avoid.

You give a last glance into the forest and can feel the rojed eyes looking for you. What was that thing? The beacon of Talus did say that a Fear Rift would make the creatures of the world aggressive and fearful. Best to stay away from it, so you start to follow the dried up creek. There is a quiet dusty village hidden amongst a grove of brozn, dying trees. “Village” is a strong word for what you see. Three rickety, run down dwellings. One has remnants of farmland backing up to the dry creek with a haggard Lizerus using a walking stick to scratch at the ground. They have warm norangia skin crested with light yemillow tips on the small scaly spikes and fins that protrude from their various joints and facial features. Their clothes are very dusty and hang off of them as a sign of malnourishment.

You are about to approach the Lizerus, but stop short as you wonder what you would even say. Does this avatar have a name? Who are you? With this thought, a feeling bubbles deep inside you and the name of your true self floats into your mind. What can you tell this being about who you are and why you are here? Macheventa mentioned these were a primitive folk, so you decide to say that you are a traveler new to this area which is quite an understatement, but true nonetheless.

As you are working out who you are, the Lizerus grunts and falls to one knee. You instinctively rush over to their aid, causing them to jump and fall over again at your sudden appearance.

“OH! Don’t sneak up on us old folk. Nearly sent me straight to Icosa. Here help me to my feet,” the old Lizerus wheezes to you.

As you help them to their feet, they thank you and introduce themselves as Cecil Rose, the last caretaker of Farluck Farms. While limping to the farmhouse, you see deep, bleck, seeping claw marks on their right leg. You set Cecil down on a chair before they speak again.

"Thank you for your assistance, those plague gremlins will be the death of me," Cecil says pointing to their leg wounds. "Would you continue your kindness and fill some bowls with water from that barrel over there for us?"

There are a couple of bowls and a ladle on a small table next to a barrel in the corner of this cozy home. The inside is much nicer than the exterior suggests, with a small fireplace, sturdy wooden table and chairs, as well as a few parched plants placed thoughtfully throughout the dwelling. Upon dipping the ladle in the barrel, it is completely dry.

Cecil gives a sigh, “"Curses! I forgot. I was getting water from the well just through the other side of the farm, when I was attacked by one of those wretched plague gremlins. Dropped both my water buckets and barely made it back here. That was last rotation and I am starting to feel a bit woozy. Would you please help an old timer by finding those buckets, filling them, and bringing them back here so us and the crops can have some refreshments? I have a feeling you can handle those gremlins a bit better than I."

You agree to help Cecil, and they give you more detailed directions to the well while thanking you again for your kindness.

“Thanks again. I will prepare the ingredients for some charbean soup for your return,” Cecil says as they gingerly get up from their chair and hobble over to a cabinet searching through various jars of spices.

You follow the Lizerus’ directions, walking a path out of the abandoned town into a thicket of thin trees and rocks. Listening to your footsteps crunching, interrupting the clicking of a group of tiny lifeforms, you wonder if the creature that attacked you in the forest was a plague gremlin. If so, you are not sure how to handle things if you run into another one. At least this time you are a little more prepared for what is out there.

A clearing opens up after only walking for a few minticks and you see a mound of carefully structured stones with an opening on top and a couple of buckets toppled over next to it. A rope is attached to one of them so you grab that bucket and start to lower it into the well when it becomes eerily silent and you sense a presence behind you. What you assume is a plague gremlin stalks you slowly with narrowing rojed eyes. You feel something inside you trying to reach out to this creature. A hum, a low frequency starts to vibrate out of your mouth but then the gremlin hisses and spits a glob of bleck ooze at your face. Your vibration ends and you skillfully dodge the ooze that splats and sizzles on a tree behind you, while in the same motion swing the bucket attached to the rope into the head of the gremlin leaping toward you.

The bucket explodes with a loud splintering crack and the plague gremlin shrieks before scurrying deeper into the woods. Well there goes one bucket. You look around to make sure there are no more gremlins, or worse, lurking in the woods and tie the rope to the remaining bucket. You pull the water up, drink your fill, splash some on your face and head before filling it again and carefully making your way back to Farluck Farm.

You make it into Cecil's home without spilling too much of the water. There is a fire going and a table prepared with dried herbs, charbeans, and berries. You quickly give Cecil a bowl of water before putting the soup ingredients plus half the contents of the bucket into a pot by the fireplace and the rest into the water barrel. As you are nestling the tarnished cooking pot onto the fire, you detail the encounter you had at the well.

Cecil nods solemnly and takes a deep drink from his water bowl, “Those are the plague gremlins. Once they showed up, the creek stopped flowing, the crops started failing, and the folks started leaving… Or dying… My dear good friend, Treywind was taken by those monsters, or so I assume.”

You stir the ingredients, releasing a waft of pleasant aromatics from the herbs and you notice Cecil is looking at you strangely.

“There was a Soul Seer that came to visit Treywind and I, last grand rotation. They talked of a great hardship coming, but also of a great savior. Mostly Macheventa talked about the wondrous adventures that await us in this loving universe.”

You drop the ladle but quickly pick it up again at the mention of Macheventa. Cecil smiles at your reaction, rocks themselves out of their chair, and shuffles over to a small drawer to pull out some papers and a pebble.

“This stone was left here by Macheventa for Treywind and I to safeguard, along with some writings to give us comfort in hard times,” Cecil says before handing you the papers.

The first paper is titled Creator’s Creed and is written in a precise yet flowing script:

Creator of all, whose design we honor, look

upon us with favor and deliver us from fear.

Make us a pleasure to our divine teachers

and forever put truth on our lips. Deliver us

from violence and anger. Give us respect

for our elders and that which belongs to

our neighbors. Give us rotations of plentiful

pastures and fruitful flocks to gladden our

hearts. We pray for the hastening of the

coming of the promised Teacher, and we

would do your will on this world as others

do on worlds beyond.

The other paper is titled Psalm of the Lightbringer, written in the same script as the other:

In days of dryness and despair

One will come of Icosa’s heir

This hero created by love and light

Will cause the nations to unite

The dawning of a new age is near

Once the Love outshines the Fear

Cecil then hands you a small brilliant abluza marble with tiny wheck flecks inside. As soon as the cool sphere touches you, your heart surges with emotion sending shivers of Love vibrating every cell of your body while a bright abluza light fills your eyes. Your mind is briefly connected to the fabric of the universe and you can almost hear the voice from the Comet calling to you until suddenly you snap back to the farm with Cecil laughing at the look on your face.

“Ha Ha, looks like I gave it to the right being, I suppose. Macheventa said that Star Sphere is part of a holy medallion. Treywind believed the medallion to be in the caves where the creek flows from, and that is why Macheventa kept the sphere here while searching for the other ones. Sadly, I believe Trey met their demise trying to find the medallion. They so wanted to be the Lightbringer, but alas I think their purpose may have been to pave the way for you.”

Both of you jump as the hiss of the pot boiling over breaks through the revelatory conversation. How lucky it was that you happen to arrive here. Was it luck or design? Hopefully you can find the medallion to complete your first mission and maybe finally get some answers. You grab the pot from the fire to cool before ladling out some fragrant charbean soup and sitting at the table. The soup has flavors that you have never imagined before and cannot even properly describe. Cecil reminisces about the times and conversations had with Machy (what they called Macheventa) and Trey, unraveling the mysteries of the universe, until Cecil starts looking a bit sleepy.

“You better get to that medallion, and I better get some rest,” Cecil says with heavy eyelids. “Just follow the creek bed to the caves and remember to go with the love of Icosa and the universe in your heart. Love be with you, Lightbringer.”

You thank Cecil for everything and venture out of the farm with a bounce in your step, a belly full of soup, and a heart full of love to take on the challenge of finding the Universe Medallion.
